Louis Wilken — horticulturist and inventor of Vertipots.

Louis Wilken

Based in Pretoria, with over forty years in horticulture. Vertipots was designed to raise plant density, reduce medium waste, and make root health visible — without the drawbacks of shared grow bags.

The system is patented and supplied for commercial tunnels as well as smaller installations.
