System

Structure, irrigation, root monitoring and comparison with grow bags.

Columns of individual pots

Up to eight levels. Plants remain separate for disease control and selective replacement. Media and pots are reusable.

  • 32 plants per square metre
  • Even irrigation top to bottom
  • Root inspection without crop disturbance
  • Elevated, clean harvest height

Water and filtration

Manifolds control supply to tunnels. Filtration supports consistent delivery and efficient use of water and nutrients.

Root health

Dense white roots and mycorrhizal association support efficient nutrient uptake. Individual pots limit the spread of root disease.
