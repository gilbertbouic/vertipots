Key advantages

Density

Up to 32 plants per m² — roughly four times a typical grow-bag layout under the same cover.

Isolation

Each plant in its own pot. Remove disease or change crop without disturbing neighbours.

Irrigation

Even delivery across levels, with drainage managed for efficiency and plant health.

How it works

Individual pots stack on a central column. Water and nutrients are delivered precisely. Any pot can be lifted for root checks or replacement.
