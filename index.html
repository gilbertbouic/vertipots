Why Commercial Growers Choose Vertipots

Engineered to solve the real problems of conventional tunnel and bag systems.

📈

4× Higher Density

32 plants per m² versus 8–12 in traditional grow-bags. More plants under the same expensive covered structure.

🛡️

Disease Isolation

Each plant lives in its own pot. One diseased plant is simply lifted out and replaced — no whole-bag loss.

💧

Even Irrigation

Pressure-compensated button drippers open only at 2 bar. Every pot, top to bottom, receives the same volume.

♻️

Reusable Pots

No single-use plastic bags. Pots are cleaned and refilled. Media + pot cost ≈ R2.80 per plant.

🔄

Zero Downtime Crop Change

Finished pots out, new crop pots in. The structure stays. Income stream continues while next plants establish in the nursery.

👷

Ergonomic Harvesting

Fruit at comfortable height. Purpose-designed trolleys. Spray gantry operated from outside the tunnel.
